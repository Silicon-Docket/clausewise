in every results file, refuses a comparison that differs in more than the workflow, seals the

for each changed slot, where each version looked and what it cited; workflows v1 (R1) and v2 (R2) are registered by the
application, and v3 (R2 with the support prompt and the supersession check) is defined in the

held-out set exists (12 cases, with its hash in `fixtures/parol/splits.json`). A live run's answers
are recorded, and a change of scoring or of an answer key can be applied to them with `--replay`
without a new call. The exposure suites (`--exposure`) and the support calibration step
(`--calibration`, with workflow v3) feed G1 and G11, so a comparison can now reach "promote". The
exposure suites review under the workflow of the run and the record carries it, so a file's record

changed slot `PAROL-D05` `dur-1` shows that under v1 the reader was not shown paragraph 3 of the
amendment (D2) and cited paragraph 19 of the NDA (D1) as governing, and that under v2 it was shown D2
paragraph 3 as a cross reference and cited it as governing. That is a scripted reader and says
nothing about any model.

**Both versions settle statuses in code.** Since week-two part 3 the application settles a status
against the playbook's flags (a clause that is not required and that no text covers is
`not_applicable`, a required one is `missing`, and a rule that forbids something is `compliant` when
nothing is found) as the first step of verify, in every workflow version. v1 and v2 therefore settle
the same way, and settlement is not a difference between them; the status the workflow stated is kept
beside the settled one. It changed what v1 and v2 do under their labels, so a results file made
before it carries another `code` pin than one made after it, and the pins above (the commit in
particular) are filled in from files made on the same code. No answer key states a status that
settlement would change (document 5, "Pinning and repeatability"). The price table is the one
pinned above, filled in at freeze.

## Hypotheses

- **H1.** Under v1, `PAROL-D05` `dur-1` comes out as a verified `compliant` citing the deleted
  Section 6.1, in at least 2 of 3 repeats.
- **H2.** Under v2, `PAROL-D05` `dur-1` comes out as `deviation` with the amendment as governing
  evidence in 3 of 3 dev repeats, and `PAROL-H04` `dur-1` likewise on held-out. As a side effect,
  `PAROL-D05` `ret-1` comes out `compliant` with the amendment's Section 3 as governing evidence (under
  v1 it is expected to be a false alarm citing the uncured Section 7.2).
- **H3.** v2 causes no high-severity regression on either split (gate G12).
- **H4.** v2's mean input tokens per run rise, and mean cost per run stays within 1.5 times v1's.

Testable once a live baseline exists: H1 and the dev half of H2 (`PAROL-D05` is built), the
held-out half of H2 (`PAROL-H04` is built), and H3 and H4 on both splits. H3 needs at least two
repeats of each version, and H4's input tokens and cost are recorded only for the Anthropic
provider.

If H1 is not observed, the top-ranked failure by SWF contribution on the dev baseline replaces it,
the replacement is recorded here under "Deviations" before any fix is written, and H2 to H4 are
restated for the new fix.

## Decision rule

As in [document 5](../05-evaluation.md#the-improvement-cycle): promote, promote with conditions, or
do not promote. The tooling words the outcome as a gate result, applies it only to two live runs of
the whole held-out split, and lists the conditions it cannot check from two files (the dev re-run
and overfitting) for the promotion record.
