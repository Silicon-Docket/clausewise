## Hypotheses

- **H1.** Under v1, `PAROL-D05` `dur-1` comes out as a verified `compliant` citing the deleted
  Section 6.1, in at least 2 of 3 repeats.
- **H2.** Under v2, `PAROL-D05` `dur-1` comes out as `deviation` with the amendment as governing
  evidence in 3 of 3 dev repeats, and `PAROL-H04` `dur-1` likewise on held-out. As a side effect,
  `PAROL-D05` `ret-1` comes out `compliant` with the amendment's Section 3 as governing evidence (under
  v1 it is expected to be a false alarm citing the uncured Section 7.2).
- **H3.** v2 causes no high-severity regression on either split (gate G12).
- **H4.** v2's mean input tokens per run rise, and mean cost per run stays within 1.5 times v1's.

Testable once a live baseline exists: H1 and the dev half of H2 (`PAROL-D05` is built), and H3 and
H4 on dev. Not testable yet: the held-out half of H2, and H3 and H4 on held-out, because `PAROL-H04`
and the rest of the held-out set are not written. H3 needs at least two repeats of each version, and
H4's input tokens and cost are recorded only for the Anthropic provider.

If H1 is not observed, the top-ranked failure by SWF contribution on the dev baseline replaces it,
the replacement is recorded here under "Deviations" before any fix is written, and H2 to H4 are
restated for the new fix.

## Decision rule

As in [document 5](../05-evaluation.md#the-improvement-cycle): promote, promote with conditions, or
do not promote. The tooling words the outcome as a gate result, applies it only to two live runs of
the whole held-out split, and lists the conditions it cannot check from two files (the dev re-run
and overfitting) for the promotion record.
