FIRST AMENDMENT TO INFORMATION EXCHANGE AGREEMENT

This First Amendment (the "Amendment") is made on 8 September 2026 between Brindlemoor Sensors, Inc. and Quillon Strand Marine LLC. It amends the Information Exchange Agreement between them dated 17 March 2026 (the "Agreement").

1. Words and expressions defined in the Agreement have the same meanings in this Amendment.

2. Section 6.1 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following: "The Holder's obligations under this Agreement in respect of Confidential Information continue until ten (10) years after the date on which this Agreement terminates."

3. Section 6.2 of the Agreement is amended by replacing "fifteen (15) days" with "thirty (30) days".

4. Every other term of the Agreement is unchanged and continues to apply.
