AMENDMENT TO CONFIDENTIALITY AND INFORMATION SHARING AGREEMENT

This Amendment (the "Amendment") is made on 15 June 2026 between Tolliver Marsh Biofuels, Inc. and Hartsgrove Kiln Works LLC. It amends the Confidentiality and Information Sharing Agreement between them dated 24 February 2026 (the "Agreement").

1. Words defined in the Agreement have the same meanings in this Amendment.

2. Section 6.1 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following: "Within forty-five (45) days after the Disclosing Party asks in writing, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy the Confidential Information it holds. It may keep copies that the law obliges it to keep and copies held in routine backup systems, and every copy so kept remains subject to this Agreement."

3. All other provisions of the Agreement stay as they were.
